## How 4.1 became measurable at all

`firmware/layering.toml` allows `ui/` exactly one dependency: `hal/`. No radio, no flash,
**no clock**. A screen is thus a function from a struct to a byte array, and "has anything
changed?" becomes a byte comparison **after** drawing instead of a guess before it.

That is exactly the difference 4.1's note describes — *"a redraw triggered by a value that
technically changed"*. A screen that could read `millis()` would draw two different images
from the same state. `app/view.cpp` therefore resolves everything time-relative into seconds
of age before it reaches the model; the screens never see a clock.

On the host this is a counter instead of an hour of watching: 3600 passes with a frozen
model, across **all five** screens, and none of them may trigger anything.

## What the measurement on the device found — three times

The first run was **not** a pass, and every step towards it was a real finding.

### 7 partials in 10 seconds

The uptime counted up second by second, `formatAge` turned it into "11s", "12s", "13s" — and
a redraw every second. Literally the bug the gate describes, in our own screen.

### Then one every 30 seconds

The battery voltage flipped between **4910 and 4903 mV** across a centivolt boundary.
Seven millivolts of ADC noise, extrapolated to **120 redraws per hour** for a number that
never moved.

Gate 1.6 allows the ADC ±50 mV anyway. Two decimal places thus claimed a precision that does
not exist. `app::buildViewModel` now quantises the **displayed** voltage to 50 mV; the raw
value still goes on the wire, because `CLAUDE.md` §2.2 requires millivolts and the server
should be able to compute a discharge curve.

### Then one per minute

The uptime again, this time at the transition "<1m" → "1m" → "2m". For a field that
`CLAUDE.md` §3.3 **does not require at all** on the `STATUS` screen:

> `STATUS` (battery, link quality per peer, unread count, duty cycle budget remaining)

It is gone. What §3.3 does require and was missing — **link quality per peer** — is now
there. The uptime travels in the `TELEMETRY` payload (§2.2), where it is read.

All other age displays are coarse (`formatAgeCoarse`, never seconds). The one exception is
the age of the GNSS fix on `POSITION`: this screen is only in front of someone who wants to
know exactly that, and the module draws tens of milliamps meanwhile — against which a
325 ms refresh is not the expensive item.

## The second finding: the refreshed area

Gate 1.1 failed on the first attempt with **471 ms**. Not noise:

| Area | Duration |
|---|---|
| whole panel as a partial window | **471 ms** |
| 152 × 24 (sketch 08) | 323 ms |
| area actually changed | **324 ms** |

`hal::DisplaySsd1681::present()` initially always pushed 200 × 200 to the controller. A
correct measurement of the wrong thing — no screen ever changes the whole area.

`hal::Canvas::diffBounds()` now returns the rectangle in which two canvases differ, rounded
outwards to byte columns (the SSD1681 addresses its RAM byte-wise). **One** pass over the
buffers answers both questions: *whether* something has changed and *where*.

## What the host checks and what it cannot

252 test cases, about 60 of them for `ui/` and `app/view`. Among other things, they check:

- the same model renders the same pixels — the prerequisite for 4.1
- an hour of idling across all five screens results in zero refreshes
- the three waiting states from §2.4 look different — the core of gate 4.4
- the feedback on a long press appears **before** the action, never after it (§3.2)
- no screen draws into the band before the footer marks, even with the fullest model
- a full refresh takes the whole area, a partial only what changed

**What cannot be checked is whether a screen *looks right*.** Those are gates 4.2 and 4.3,
and they need the panel and eyes.

## Open

- **4.2** Go through all five screens with and without a peer. Needs a second device for
  half of it.
- **4.3** Feedback on a long press — the order is host-checked, the *appearance* is not.
- **4.4** The three delivery states on the device. Needs a second device.
- **Power menu:** the controls are finished and tested, but `EnterSleep` and `Shutdown`
  are **returned, not executed**. What "sleep" means is the state machine from §3.1 and
  therefore Phase 5; what "shutdown" means on this board is not settled at all, per D2 and
  D13.
